01 · Brand

The idea

DelChaos is HR and workforce software that a company owns outright: attendance, payroll, leave, onboarding and chat in one system, with the full source code. The logo is a person standing on the letters d and c. The two letters share one stroke, and the person is the stem of the d. Order is built from people, not from rules.

What the mark says

  • The person is the employee the product exists for
  • d and c touch: the name is one shape
  • The collar and tie keep it professional, the round letters keep it human
  • Shared stroke: everything in the system is connected

How the brand feels

  • Calm: lots of space, one bright colour
  • Sharp: exact geometry, plain words
  • Human: round forms, people first
  • Playful: a little wit about the word "chaos"

05 · Colour in use

Approved pairings

Contrast ratios are measured. Lime on white is too weak to read, so on light pages the lime person is a shape only. Use black-only for anything small, and deep lime when lime must act as text or a line.

AaWhite on Ink19.8:1 · AA, any size
AaLime on Ink14.8:1 · AA, any size
AaInk on Lime14.8:1 · AA, any size
AaInk on White19.8:1 · AA, any size
AaDeep lime on White2.4:1 · never for text or small marks
AaLime on White1.5:1 · never for text or small marks

Backgrounds

On photographs, place the white or black one-colour logo over a calm, even area. If the area is busy, set the logo on a solid Ink or Lime tile.

07 · Misuse

What not to do

The logo is a precise drawing. Do not change it.

✕ Stretched or squashed
✕ Rotated
✕ Off-palette colour
✕ Shadow or glow
✕ Low contrast
✕ Busy background
  • Do not rearrange the person, the d or the c, or resize them separately
  • Do not remove the tie on the full symbol: use the small-size cut instead
  • Do not use the lime person on white at small sizes: use black-only
  • Do not type "DelChaos" yourself and place it beside the symbol: use the lockup files

08 · Voice and use

Voice

Plain, warm and a little dry. Talk about people and work, not "solutions". One joke about chaos is enough.

Before you ship anything

  • Is it one of the supplied files, unchanged?
  • Is there 2x clear space on every side?
  • Is it large enough (see page 4)?
  • Does the colour pairing appear on page 6?
  • Trademark: no clearance search has been done. Run one before filing or launching widely.
