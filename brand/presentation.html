The brief

HR and workforce operations software: attendance, payroll, leave, onboarding and chat in one system. Sold as a complete product with its full source code, for teams that want to own it. It should feel calm, sharp, human and a little playful, never corporate-stiff.

The dc person

A person in a collar and tie stands on dc: the d and c share one stroke, and the person is the d's stem.

  • The person is the employee the software exists for
